Before you begin

Have these details ready.

The more complete your information, the faster the Ministry can process your registration.

  • A valid email address and phone number

    Needed for account verification and status updates.

  • Your location in Kano State

    Local Government Area and ward of residence or operation.

  • Organisation or club details

    For groups and clubs: leadership contacts and a short description of your work.

  • Supporting documents, where applicable

    Any documents required by your chosen pathway, as listed in the portal.

Questions

Good to know.

Answers to the questions we hear most often about registration.

Ask us something else
Is registration really free?

Yes. Registration with the Ministry is free. The Ministry will never ask you to pay to register through this website. Only use the official portal links provided on this page.

Who can register?

Young people resident in Kano State, youth groups and associations, sports clubs and eligible applicants for Ministry empowerment programmes. Specific eligibility requirements are shown on each registration pathway.

Can I save my application and come back later?

Yes. Once you create an account in the portal, you can save a draft application and return to it later using your login details.

What happens after I submit?

You will receive an application reference and status updates. Ministry officers review submissions and may contact you if any clarification or correction is needed.

Do I need a registered business or NGO certificate?

Not necessarily. The Ministry recognises that many valuable youth groups operate informally. Required documents are listed on the relevant pathway and are applied according to Ministry policy.
