Our introduction

A Ministry built around young people.

We are responsible for youth development and sports in Kano State — connecting young people, community organisations and sports structures to opportunity, support and a stronger future.

Youth

Skills, education, enterprise and civic pathways for every young person.

Sports

Grassroots participation, club development and elite performance.

“To develop Kano State youth with equal opportunities to realise their dreams and aspirations, irrespective of their background or standard of living.”

Hon. Ibrahim Abdullahi Waiya
