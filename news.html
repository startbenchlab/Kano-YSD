Featured story

Kano Pillars visit the Governor

The club presented its season achievements, community plans and youth pathway programme to the State Government.

Kano Pillars were received at the Government House for a formal visit celebrating the club’s continued contribution to football in Kano State and its work with young players across the city.

During the visit, officials outlined plans for stronger grassroots scouting, improved training support and closer collaboration with schools and community clubs. The Ministry will continue to work alongside the club to create clear pathways from local pitches to elite competition.
