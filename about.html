Our introduction

Welcome to the Ministry of Youth and Sports Development.

We are the Kano State Ministry responsible for youth development and sports. Our work connects young people, community organisations, sports structures and partners to the support, opportunity and recognition they need to thrive.

Our policy focuses on increasing youth employment, education, health, physical development and moral uprightness. We provide entrepreneurial training as a means of creating real employment opportunities for young people across the State.

“To develop Kano State youth with equal opportunities to realise their dreams and aspirations, irrespective of their background and standard of living, in a safe and secure environment that optimises their contribution to State and national development.”

Empowering youth

Skills, education, enterprise and civic pathways for every young person.

Supporting sports

Grassroots participation, club development and elite performance.

Our direction

What guides our work.

A clear policy framework keeps our programmes focused, inclusive and accountable to the people of Kano State.

01

Policy statement

Increasing youth employment, education, health and physical development, and moral uprightness. We provide entrepreneurial training to young people as a means of creating employment opportunities.

02

Policy vision

To develop Kano State youth with equal opportunities to realise their dreams and aspirations, irrespective of their background and standard of living, in a safe and secure environment that optimises their contribution to State and national development.

03

Policy mission

To provide stakeholders in youth development with an all-inclusive framework that ensures the realisation of the full potential of all Kano young men and women, irrespective of their ethnic, religious or socio-economic background.

04

Policy goal

To provide an appropriate framework that protects the fundamental human rights of all youth, promotes their optimal development and well-being, and enhances their participation in every sphere of national development.
